# Historical source reviews

These four reports are byte-preserving snapshots from the original
Legendre_k8 research directory. Their relative links and references to earlier
76-cell or 94-cell artifacts describe that original directory and version.
They are historical evidence, not the current publication package's entry
points or a new human peer review. Their SHA256 hashes are recorded in
`../source_snapshot_manifest.json`.

The current human-readable paper is `../../paper/manuscript.md`. The current
four numerical certificates are under `../../results/`. The new Lean work
postdates these reviews and has its own build and axiom audit records.
