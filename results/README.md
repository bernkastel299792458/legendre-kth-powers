# results/ — exploratory outputs (not part of the claims)

This folder holds outputs from the exploratory stages of the project. **It is not part of the claims.** The authoritative certificates are in `../proof/certificates/`, and the authoritative statements are in `../proof/manuscript.md`.

Many files here were produced with superseded inputs:
- Lee's eq. (2.7) KLN constants (`cert_hyb_k*.csv`, `all_k_hyb.txt`, `rigorous_thresholds.md`);
- the withdrawn constant 19.62 (`*_k67_*`, `*thesis*`, `*_lw*`);
- conditional hypotheses (`cert_cond_*`).

They are kept as a record of how the results were obtained. `min_k_conservative.md` explains how the minimal k = 70 / 74 was determined.
