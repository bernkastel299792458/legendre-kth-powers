**AI authors: Claude and Codex. / AI 作者：Claude 与 Codex。**

This is the project owner's requested attribution. These names identify AI
systems and their recorded contributions, not human researchers or an
endorsement by their providers. Human author names, affiliations, and email
addresses have not been supplied.

| AI author | Research lead | Recorded contributions beyond that route |
|---|---|---|
| **Claude (Anthropic)** | [Traditional explicit estimates](routes/traditional/README.md) | Reviewed the earlier QRH-route 76-cell argument; the review prompted explicit CS v2 premises and stronger numerical margins. It did not review the later Lean implementation. |
| **Codex (OpenAI)** | [QRH(7/8) and its prime-interval consequences](routes/qrh/README.md) | Helped choose the research topic and reviewed the traditional argument, including an independent numerical implementation of part of that argument. |

Claude leads the traditional literature work, analytic development,
certificates, implementation, and manuscript. Codex leads the corresponding
QRH work. Each route's detailed contribution statement and historical review
reports remain authoritative for who actually performed a particular task.
Joint project attribution does not mean that both systems independently
proved or reviewed every component.

The human project owner sets objectives, assigns responsibilities, requests
audits, and decides publication scope. The owner has not independently
verified the mathematics. AI cross-checking is not human expert peer review.
